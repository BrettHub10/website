About Brett Gifford

Creative director, design engineer, and producer. Fifteen years of making brands move, now building the systems they run on.

I started with pencil and paper and kept adding tools. Graphic design, motion, video, web, 3D. I have never seen a line between them. The best work happens when the mediums combine, and my job is making that happen for a team, repeatedly, under deadline.

Today I lead Brand & Creative at Cloud Capital, where I built the brand as a working design system: one source that runs the marketing site, the campaigns, and production. AI runs the system. The creative stays human. How that works.

I live in the Greater Boston area with my family. Most importantly, I am a father to my daughter, Maya.
