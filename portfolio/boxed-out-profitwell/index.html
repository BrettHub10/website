Branded show · Creative direction & visual execution

Boxed Out is a branded show created for ProfitWell, built around a highly designed visual system and custom set. I led the creative and visual execution, handling the visual design, set design, motion graphics, and the majority of the 3D, texturing, and tracking.

The goal was to create a distinctive, repeatable format that could scale episode to episode while maintaining a bold, cohesive look that elevated ProfitWell’s brand on camera.
